# July Regime Shift & Behavioral Feature Engineering Validated

Forensic audit established that July fraud changed mechanism: median fraud amount halved (1,636 to 1,064 BDT), night fraud dropped from 9.5% to 6.0%, and specific locations surged (LOC_033 from 4.2% to 8.7%). Strictly-prior behavioral features (location velocity, category scaling, smurfing ratios) improved Fold 3 PR-AUC from 0.4973 to 0.5206 and overall CV from 0.6923 to 0.7312 without reverse engineering or ID targeting.
